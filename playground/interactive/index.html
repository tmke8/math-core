What is this?

The math in the document below is converted by a single InteractiveDocument, which knows all math snippets of the document and the order they are in. When a paragraph is edited, the converter is told which snippets were replaced, and it answers with the snippets whose output is now a different one. Only those are updated on the page; they are highlighted for a moment.

Click on a paragraph to edit it, and click somewhere else (or press Esc or Ctrl/⌘+Enter) to render it. Write inline math as $…$ and block math as $$…$$. The + buttons add a paragraph. Some things to try:

  • Add a paragraph with a numbered equation, like $$\begin{equation} a = b \end{equation}$$, somewhere in the middle: the equations after it get new numbers, and so do the references to them, wherever they are.
  • Change the definition of \RR in the first paragraph, or remove it.
  • Rename a \label, or move a labelled equation to the other side of a reference to it.
  • Edit a formula which has no number, no label and no definition: nothing else is converted.
